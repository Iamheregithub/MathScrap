# LessonBoard 1.7.7.12

- Page thumbnails sit inside their surrounding tan frame with consistent padding.
- Documents has a labelled folder button at the start of the header; the repeated LessonBoard label is removed.
- Solid and dashed lines share one dock button. Double-click toggles the style and selects it immediately; a single click preserves the displayed style.
- Ink tool trays are anchored above their tool. Click or briefly hover to open, click again or press Escape to close, and start drawing to dismiss without consuming the stroke. Hovering alone does not select a different tool.
- Circle and Tap: with Eraser selected, draw a roughly closed loop, lift, then tap inside to erase only the enclosed portions of digital ink. Tap outside, press Escape, change pages, or switch tools to cancel. Inserted shapes, images, text, and covers are preserved. One Undo restores the erasure.
- Open eraser gestures complete on release so an enclosing loop can be recognised before any ink is removed.

The Windows build publishes a direct installer download after its native, recovery, and OpenBoard compatibility checks succeed.

Dismissing a tray keeps the selected tool, colour and thickness active. Solid and dashed lines retain their separate per-colour thickness memories.
